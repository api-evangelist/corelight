Recent cyberattacks against U.S. water and wastewater systems have put some familiar operational technology (OT) security problems back in the headlines. Federal agencies have warned about malicious actors targeting internet-facing programmable logic controllers (PLCs), changing device configurations, and disrupting operations at utilities across multiple states.
